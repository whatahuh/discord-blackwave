# BlackWave brand guide

- BlackWave TV: live TV, sports, movies and more.
- BlackWave Cinema: VOD movies and shows, previously DeadmanStreams.
- Style: hybrid modern/pirate design; deep black, electric blue and metallic gold.
- TV emblem: pirate ship, skull-and-crossbones sail, waves.
- Bot avatar: friendly pirate robot with glowing blue eyes; **no text**.
- Welcome banner: **one ship**, one instance of **BlackWave TV**, headline **WELCOME ABOARD**, and an invitation to open a ticket.
- Avoid unnecessary decorative copy, duplicate wording and crowded ship imagery.
- Use simplified icons for circular Discord avatars; detailed artwork for banners and ads.
